# M0 physics ladder comparison

Generated by `scripts/08_compare_physics_ladder.py`. Formulation, validation and
interpretation are in [`docs/propagation_physics.md`](../../../docs/propagation_physics.md).

The rungs are M0 (timing only), M0-TD+ (+ spreading + 10 dB/m attenuation),
M0-FD (the same physics in the frequency domain), M0-FD+D (+ frequency-dependent
directivity) and M0-FD+D+A (+ attenuation ∝ f).

## Key numbers

- M0-FD vs M0-TD+: maximum relative difference 8.5e-14. The two domains are
  identical.
- Edge/centre pulse-echo amplitude: M0 0 dB; with spreading −1.05 dB; with
  directivity as well −2.07 dB.
- TFM: the peak is exactly at (0, 40) mm for every rung. The lateral −6 dB width
  goes from 1.222 mm (M0) to 1.244 mm (full ladder); the axial width stays at
  0.30 mm.
- Frequency dependence of directivity (M0-FD+D vs directivity frozen at 5 MHz):
  0.6 % FMC change and 0.5 % TFM change; edge centroid −10 kHz.
- Attenuation of 10 dB/m with n = 1: −4 kHz centroid shift and a 0.6 % TFM
  change. It only becomes significant above about 200 dB/m (see
  `figures/attenuation_sensitivity.png`).
- RMS dB error of the gated amplitude against experiment, with dead or weak
  channels 4, 40, 46 and 52 excluded: 6.13 dB (M0), 5.89 dB (with spreading),
  5.67 dB (with directivity as well). Treat this with caution: the experimental
  ROI response is 26.6 mm wide and is not a confirmed hole echo.
